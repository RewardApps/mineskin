MINESKIN

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026 · App: MineSkin (Android, package com.snscoders.mineskin)

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
  • Your favorites, downloads and settings stay on your device.
  • The app uses Google Firebase to load the catalog, measure how the app is used and report crashes.
  • Future versions may show ads (Google AdMob) and offer optional in-app purchases (Google Play Billing). Section 5 explains what that involves.
  • The skins, maps and packs in the catalog come from publicly available servers. If you own content and want it removed, email sandipvai4456@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

MineSkin is an independent app for browsing and downloading Minecraft skins, maps, texture packs, add-ons, buildings and seeds. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean the MineSkin developer. You can contact us at sandipvai4456@gmail.com.

MineSkin is not an official Minecraft product. It is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

None. MineSkin has no sign-up, login or profile, and it never asks you to type in personal information. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply.

App usage (Firebase Analytics)

To understand which content is popular and improve the app, the app records anonymous usage events through Google Analytics for Firebase:

  • Which screens and tabs you open.
  • Which items you view, download or add to favorites (item ID, title and category).
  • Search terms you enter in the app's search box.

Firebase also collects standard technical information automatically, such as an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, language, approximate country (derived from your IP address) and session information. This information isn't linked to your name or contact details.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report. It contains the technical error details, device model, operating system version, app version and an installation identifier. It's used only to find and fix bugs.

Content downloads

When the catalog loads or you download an item, your device requests files from our catalog database (Google Cloud Firestore), our image host (Firebase Hosting) and our file hosting server. Like any web server, these services can see your device's IP address and the files requested, and may keep them in short-lived server logs.

3. Information stored only on your device

The following stays on your phone and is never uploaded by MineSkin:

  • Your favorites list and app settings (such as dark mode).
  • Downloaded skins, maps and packs, kept so they work offline.
  • Files you import from your device. They're copied into the app's private storage and never uploaded.

When you share or export an item, or open it with Minecraft, Android's share and open features pass the file to the app you choose. We don't receive it.

4. How we use information

  • To deliver the catalog and the files you download.
  • To see which content and features are used, so we can improve the catalog and the app.
  • To find and fix crashes and errors.
  • If ads or in-app purchases are introduced: to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and deliver and restore purchases.

We don't sell your personal information.

5. Advertising and in-app purchases

The current version has no ads and no purchases. Future versions may add either or both. If they do, the following applies.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads would be provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it, for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change that choice in the app, and without consent you'll see only non-personalized ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings. See how Google uses information for ads.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases, such as removing ads or unlocking extra content, would be processed by Google Play. Your payment details go to Google, not to us. We'd receive only what's needed to deliver and verify a purchase: the product, a purchase token, the order ID, the purchase time and its status. We may store this information to restore your purchases and prevent fraud. Refunds and billing questions follow Google Play's refund policies.

We'll update this policy before either feature launches if it differs from what's described here.

6. Third-party services

MineSkin uses these Google services, which process data under their own terms:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. See the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Cloud Firestore and Firebase Hosting, for the catalog and preview images.
  • If introduced: Google AdMob for ads (see the Google Privacy Policy) and Google Play Billing for purchases (see the Google Payments Privacy Notice).

Downloadable files are delivered from a third-party web hosting server.

7. Where the content comes from

The skins, maps, texture packs, add-ons, buildings and seeds in MineSkin were collected from publicly available servers. Some preview images and skin names are generated automatically, and a small number of skins were created with AI image tools. All trademarks and content belong to their respective owners.

Report content: If you believe an item infringes your copyright or other rights, or is inappropriate, email sandipvai4456@gmail.com with the item name and a short explanation. We'll review it and remove it where appropriate.

8. Data retention

Analytics data is kept according to the retention settings of our Google Analytics property. Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for a limited period, currently about 90 days. If purchases are introduced, purchase records are kept as long as needed to provide the purchased features and meet legal and tax requirements. Ad data is kept by Google under its own policies. Data on your device stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall MineSkin.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete local data: clear MineSkin's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. This also resets the analytics identifier on your device.
  • Limit tracking and ads: you can reset or delete your Android advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in your device settings. If ads are introduced, you'll also be able to change your ad consent choice in the app.
  • Requests: to ask about, access or delete data related to your use of the app, email sandipvai4456@gmail.com. Because we don't collect names or emails in the app, we may need details such as your device model and when you used the app to locate records.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have additional rights, such as access, correction, deletion and objection. Contact us to use them.

10. Children

MineSkin is intended for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and any future ads will not be personalized for users known to be under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Security

Connections to our services use encrypted HTTPS, and downloaded files are checked against their expected checksums before use. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect the information the app handles.

12. Permissions

The app currently requests only internet access, to load the catalog and download content. If ads are added, it may also use Android's advertising ID permission; purchases use Google Play. It doesn't request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos. Files you import are chosen by you through Android's file picker.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
